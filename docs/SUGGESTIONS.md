# Features Knot'ily doesn't have yet

Nothing here is built. Pick what you want (I can show a demo first). Effort: S = a day or two, M = about a week, L = more.

## Selling and checkout
| # | Feature | Why | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Discount codes and gift cards** at checkout | Launch offers, influencer codes, festive sales. | M |
| 2 | **Cash on delivery** (with a fee and OTP check on the phone) | Many first-time Indian buyers only pay COD. More returns-to-origin. | M |
| 3 | **Quick add** from product cards (pick a size without opening the product) | Faster shopping on phones. | S |
| 4 | **Add to bag** next to Buy now (keep shopping instead of going straight to checkout) | Bigger orders. | S |
| 5 | **Gift wrap and gift note** | Gifting season. | S |
| 6 | **PIN code check** on the product page (delivery date, city/state auto-fill at checkout) | Fewer address mistakes, delivery confidence. | S |
| 7 | **International shipping** and prices in other currencies | Diaspora customers. | L |
